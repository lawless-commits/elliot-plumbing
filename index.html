            Frequently asked questions

            I have a burst pipe. What should I do right now?

            Close the main water valve (usually near the meter or where the supply enters the property). If water is near plugs or the DB board, switch off that circuit. If the geyser is involved, switch off its breaker and close the geyser's cold-water isolation valve. Then WhatsApp us a photo and your suburb.

            Should I repair or replace my geyser?

            Faulty elements, thermostats, safety valves and vacuum breakers can usually be repaired. A geyser that is leaking from the tank itself, or is badly corroded, normally needs replacing. We'll tell you which after inspection and explain why.

            My toilet keeps running or a tap drips. Is it worth fixing?

            Yes. A running toilet can waste thousands of litres a month, and that shows up on your water bill. Most cistern and tap repairs are quick and inexpensive compared to the water they waste.

            Do you do new installations?

            Yes: taps, mixers, toilets, basins, geysers and general plumbing for renovations, kitchens and bathrooms. Send your plan or photos on WhatsApp for a quote.

            How fast can you get to Kyalami, Midrand or Johannesburg?

            Kyalami and Midrand are our home ground, so urgent jobs there are prioritised for same-day callouts where possible. Wider Johannesburg depends on location and the day's bookings. WhatsApp is the fastest way to get a time.

            Is the estimator price what I'll pay?

            No. It's a rough, non-binding guide. We confirm the price after seeing the job and before any work starts, so there are no surprises.
